Fall lawn care.Good questions.

Fall is more than putting the mower away and hoping for the best. A few sensible moves now make spring less of a yard-related apology tour.

When should I stop mowing my lawn in Sioux Falls?

Keep mowing as needed while the grass is growing, rather than picking a final date months ahead. A warm fall can keep the mower busy longer; cold weather can shut things down sooner. Avoid cutting frozen or soggy turf, and do not scalp the lawn for winter. Keep a sensible height for your grass type and clear heavy leaf buildup. The calendar is a suggestion. Actual growing conditions get the final vote.

Can I mulch fall leaves instead of raking them?

Yes, if you can chop them finely enough that the pieces settle into the grass instead of burying it. A light scattering is very different from a soggy leaf lasagna. If grass is disappearing underneath, remove the excess or move suitable leaves into landscape beds. Mulch regularly as leaves fall rather than trying to defeat the whole season in one pass. Your lawn still needs light and air beneath the autumn aesthetic.

Further reading: UMN Turfgrass Science: fall lawn Q&A ↗

19 second read

Is fall a good time to fix thin spots in a South Dakota lawn?

Late summer into early fall is a strong window for improving cool-season lawns. SDSU highlights roughly mid-August through early October for important late-season care, but seeding needs enough time to establish before hard freezes. Do not read that as permission to scatter seed the night before a blizzard. Match the timing to the grass and weather, get seed in contact with soil, and plan for consistent moisture while it establishes.
