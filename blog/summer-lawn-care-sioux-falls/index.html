Summer lawn care.Good questions.

July can make a lawn look personally offended. A taller cut, smarter watering and a little restraint go a long way for the cool-season grass common around Sioux Falls.

How high should I cut my grass in summer?

For the bluegrass and fescue lawns common in South Dakota, aim for 3 inches or taller during summer. Taller grass shades the soil and handles heat better than a lawn shaved down to the dirt. During severe heat or drought, mow only when the grass actually needs it. Cutting it shorter to buy another week between mows sounds efficient. The stressed-out lawn and extra weed opportunities are the catch.

What is the best time of day to water my lawn?

Early morning is the sweet spot, generally around 5 to 9 a.m. Less water disappears to midday heat and wind, and the grass can dry afterward. Evening watering can leave blades wet longer and encourage disease. Count rainfall before running the sprinklers, and check current local watering rules. Your sidewalk does not need a drink, either. Adjust sprinkler coverage instead of paying to irrigate concrete.

20 second read

Is my brown summer lawn dead or just dormant?

Brown grass is not automatically dead grass. Cool-season lawns can go dormant during hot, dry weather, but disease, insects and other damage can also cause brown patches. Look at the pattern and conditions before declaring the whole yard a loss. Avoid mowing turf that has stopped growing, limit traffic, and investigate patches that keep worsening or fail to recover when cooler, wetter conditions return. Guessing and dumping products on it is an expensive hobby.
